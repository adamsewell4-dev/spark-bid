Connect your studio

The control panel talks to your recording software over your own computer — nothing leaves this machine.

  1. Open OBS Studio and leave it running.
  2. In the top menu choose Tools → WebSocket Server Settings.
  3. Tick Enable WebSocket server, then click Show Connect Info to see the password.
  4. Type that password in below and press Connect.

Your setup video walks through this step as well.
